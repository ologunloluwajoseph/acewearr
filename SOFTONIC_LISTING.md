## Full Description

AceWears — Threads Reimagined

The ultimate fashion e-commerce app for the modern wardrobe. Shop premium apparel with cutting-edge AI features, interest-free credit, and circular trade-in.

★ AI VIRTUAL TRY-ON (Premium)
See how any garment looks on YOU before buying. Upload a photo, set your skin tone and hair, and our AI renders a photorealistic visualization. Works for clothes, shoes, jewelry, and wigs.

★ BUY ON CREDIT — INTEREST FREE
Your credit balance grows with every purchase. Shop now, pay later, no interest, no fees, no catch.

★ SHOPPABLE ACE REELS
Swipe through curated short videos of our latest drops. Tap any product for 1-tap add to cart.

★ WISHLIST + SOCIAL SHARING
Save your favorites and share your wishlist on Facebook, WhatsApp, X/Twitter. Download a beautiful share image.

★ PAYSTACK CHECKOUT
Secure payments via Paystack. Visa, Mastercard, Verve, bank transfer.

★ MULTI-VENDOR MARKETPLACE
Become a seller and list your own products. 90% revenue share with weekly payouts.

★ LOYALTY POINTS
Earn points for purchases, reviews, and shares. Climb tiers from Bronze to Platinum for exclusive perks.

★ 11 LANGUAGES, 4 CURRENCIES
English, Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo, French, Spanish, German, Chinese, Portuguese, Arabic, Swahili.
Pay in USD, NGN, EUR, or GBP.

★ DARK MODE
Beautiful dark theme for night shopping.

★ OFFLINE READY
Browse products offline. Cart syncs when you reconnect.
